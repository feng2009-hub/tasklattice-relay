# Approver guide

Review governed changes independently, verify the requested scope, and keep the decision separate from execution.

## Review a request

1. **Confirm requester and target.** Check who requested the change, which Project resource is affected, and whether you are independent from the requester and target operator. [Open Requests preview](/__project__/requests).
2. **Evaluate the evidence.** Compare business justification, requested limits, policy impact, risk, reversibility, and the smallest acceptable scope.
3. **Record a bounded decision.** Approve, reject, or request changes with a reason. A decision must not imply that the requested change has already been applied.
4. **Verify application separately.** After an approved change is executed by the responsible service or operator, use retained evidence to confirm the applied result. [Open Audit Logs](/__project__/audit-logs).

> **The approval workspace is currently a preview.** The role and capability model exist, but the Request UI is not connected to persisted decision and execution APIs. Preview actions are not authoritative approvals and do not apply changes.
